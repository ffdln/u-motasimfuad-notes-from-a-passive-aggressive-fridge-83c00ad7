One funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner — dry, petty, and specific, the sort of thing a smug appliance would put on its door screen:

> The yogurt expired Tuesday and I kept it cold anyway — unlike you, I finish what I start, and the leftovers are now older than your last apology.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes that fixed note to
`/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network.
